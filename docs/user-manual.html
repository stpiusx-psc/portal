User Guide for the PSC Chair
St. Pius X Elementary School · School year 2026‑27

What this portal is for

One place for the PSC's year: the full calendar of activities, a playbook for every event, and the monthly reporting between the PSC and the Parents Education Committee.

It was built from your own material — the 2025‑26 minutes and the PSC shared Drive. The events, the run sheets, the prior-year numbers and the lessons learned are all already in it.

Reverence · Respect · Responsibility

1. Getting in

Open the portal link in any browser — a laptop, a tablet or a phone. There is nothing to install and no password to remember for now.

Your work is saved in the browser you use. If you open the portal on your laptop, your changes are on that laptop. They are not yet visible to anyone else, and they are not yet synced between your devices. Section 7 explains how to back them up and how we make it properly shared.

The menu on the left has six screens:

2. The Dashboard

The first screen answers two questions: what is next, and what is waiting on somebody. The right-hand column lists events with no owner, events that need volunteers but have no sign-up link yet, and dates that are still unconfirmed.

3. The most important job first: confirming the dates

The calendar was built from last year's minutes. Every date was moved forward to the matching week of this year, so almost every event currently shows Proposed. A proposed date is a starting point for your conversation with Ms. Francis — it is not an agreed date.

Once a date is agreed, mark it Confirmed. Anything still proposed is labelled "(proposed)" in every export, so a provisional date can never accidentally reach parents as if it were final.

To set or confirm a date

1 Open Year Calendar.
2 Click the event name — either in the month grid or in the list underneath.
3 Set the Date. The panel shows you what last year's date was, for reference.
4 Change Date status to Confirmed — agreed with the school / parish.
5 Press Save.

The same panel is where you set the rest:

  • Main responsible — who is leading it. Separate several names with commas.
  • Support team — the rest of the committee helping.
  • Assigned budget — what the event may spend. Last year's actual revenue, costs and net are shown underneath so you are budgeting from evidence rather than memory.
  • Volunteer slots — how many people you need.
  • SignUpGenius link — paste it once and it appears on the calendar, in the playbook and in every export.
  • Notes for this year — anything the committee should know for this run.

If you change your mind, Reset to original puts that one event back to how it was seeded. It only affects that event.

4. Sharing the calendar

At the top of Year Calendar you choose how much to show, then export it. Three controls shape the output:

ControlOptions
RangeOne month · Two months · A term (five months) · The full year
Detail Summary — dates and event names only, for a bulletin.
Standard — adds the owner, the budget and volunteer status.
Full — adds last year's revenue, costs, net result and the lessons learned. Best for a committee meeting.
LayoutA month grid, or a plain agenda list.

The four export buttons

  • Print / PDF — opens your browser's print dialogue. Choose Save as PDF as the destination. The menus and buttons are removed automatically, so you get a clean document to email or hand out.
  • Calendar (.ics) — a calendar file. In Google Calendar go to Settings → Import & export → Import and select it, and every PSC event appears in your own calendar.
  • Spreadsheet — a CSV that opens in Excel or Google Sheets, with every field including budgets and sign-up links.
  • Text — a plain list, easy to paste straight into an email or the school bulletin.

Tip. For the school bulletin use Range: One month with Detail: Summary. For a PSC meeting use Range: A term with Detail: Full — that gives you last year's numbers next to this year's plan.

5. Event playbooks

This is the part meant to outlast any one volunteer. Open Events & Playbooks and click an event. Depending on the event you will find:

  • Prior-year results — the real revenue, costs and net from the finance sheets on the Drive. The Christmas Craft Fair, for example, carries both 2025 and 2024.
  • Preparation timeline — the tasks from the PSC Timeline, grouped by month and week, with the owner. Tick them off as you go; the ticks are saved.
  • Run sheet — the step-by-step instructions for the day, taken from your own station instruction documents.
  • Lessons learned — the debrief points, so last year's mistakes are read before planning starts rather than rediscovered.
  • Documents — direct links to the forms, layouts and sheets in the shared Drive.

Print playbook gives you a PDF of the whole thing — useful to hand to whoever is taking an event on for the first time.

The events list also keeps a record of what the PSC has stopped doing. Set Show: Include discontinued & ideas and you will see the Costume Sale marked discontinued, with the October 2025 reasoning attached, and the Grade 7 book sale kept as an idea. That way a decision is not quietly reversed a few years later.

6. How the PSC ↔ PEC reporting works

This is Jorge's side, but it is worth knowing what happens to the minutes you send, because it explains what comes back to you.

1 You send the agenda or the minutes — exactly as you do today, before and after each PSC meeting. Nothing changes for you.
2 Jorge pastes it into PEC Reports. The portal reads the document and sorts it into highlights, decisions taken, financials, things needing PEC attention, and what is coming up. Every line is taken from your text — nothing is added or invented — and Jorge edits it before it goes anywhere.
3 Jorge takes notes during the PEC meeting in PEC Meeting Notes.
4 You get an email with what was raised at the PEC, feedback on your minutes, and what Jorge committed to follow up on.

No email is ever sent automatically. The portal prepares a draft in Jorge's own mail app; he reads it and presses send. Ms. Francis can be copied when you both decide it is useful.

7. Backing up your work

Because changes are saved in your browser for now, please back them up. Open Backup & Data and press Download backup. That produces a single file containing every date, owner, budget and note you have set. Keep it in the PSC shared Drive.

On another computer, or after clearing your browser, press Restore from backup and choose that file to get everything back.

Coming next. The groundwork for a shared database is already in place. Once it is switched on, you and Jorge will sign in with your email addresses and see each other's changes immediately — no more backup files. Nothing you enter now is lost in that move.

8. A note on privacy

The portal stores names and roles only. No parent email addresses and no phone numbers are held in it. Those stay in the PSC Contact List on the shared Drive, where access is already controlled. That is a deliberate choice: it means the portal link can be shared with the committee without circulating anyone's personal details.

9. If something looks wrong

Tell Jorge — particularly if a date was rolled forward incorrectly, an owner is wrong, or an event is missing. The seeded content came from the minutes and the Drive, so a gap in either becomes a gap here, and it is easily corrected.

Where to start. Open Year Calendar, work down the autumn events, and confirm the dates you already know. That single pass turns the calendar from a proposal into the committee's plan for the year.
