Hi Lynda, hi Ms. Francis,

Over the last while I have put together something I hope will be genuinely useful to
the committee, and I wanted to show it to you both before anyone else sees it.

It is a portal that holds the PSC's year in one place:

- **The full calendar** for 2026-27 — every event, with its date, who is leading it,
  the volunteer sign-up link and the budget. It exports by month, by term or for the
  whole year, as a PDF for the bulletin, a spreadsheet, or a file you can import
  straight into Google Calendar.
- **A playbook for each event** — the run sheet, the month-by-month preparation
  list, what it raised and cost last year, the lessons learned from the debrief, and
  links to the documents already on the shared Drive.
- **The reporting between the PSC and the PEC**, which is the part I am responsible
  for and which currently lives in my inbox.

Here is the honest reason I built it. Lynda, an enormous amount of what makes these
events work is in your head and in your files, and every year that means you carry
the load of remembering it and explaining it. I wanted to take some of that off you
— so that a parent who offers to help with the Craft Fair can read what actually
needs doing instead of asking you, and so the rest of us have visibility into the
year without having to interrupt you for it. I would rather contribute this way than
add another thing to your list.

**Everything in it came from your own material** — the 2025-26 agendas and minutes
you have been sending me, and the shared Drive. I have not invented anything. Where
a date was rolled forward from last year it is clearly marked as *proposed*, so
nothing looks agreed when it is not.

**To get in:**

1. Open https://jchinchillav.github.io/St.-Pius-PSC/
2. Click "First time here? Create an account"
3. Sign up with this exact address:
   - Lynda: lyndadfreeman@gmail.com
   - Ms. Francis: cfrancis@saintpius.ca
4. Choose your own password, then click the confirmation link that arrives by email

You both have full editing access, so anything you change is saved immediately and
visible to the other. I have attached a short guide with screenshots.

**The one thing that would help most:** almost every date is still marked *proposed*,
because I took them from last year. If the two of you can confirm the autumn dates
you already know — the BBQ, the Runathon, the Christmas Craft Fair — the calendar
stops being my guess and becomes the committee's actual plan.

A couple of things I want to be upfront about. Parent contact details are
deliberately **not** stored in the portal — those stay in the contact list on the
Drive, where access is already controlled. And this is a first version: if something
is wrong, missing, or simply not how the PSC works, please tell me and I will fix it.
It is only worth having if it makes your work lighter rather than heavier.

Thank you both, and thank you for everything you already do for the school.

Jorge Chinchilla
PEC Liaison, St. Pius X Parent Standing Committee
